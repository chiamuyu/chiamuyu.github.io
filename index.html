I am a professor at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. My research interests span AI safety and security, computer and network security, and data privacy.

I study adversarial and backdoor threats to learning systems—including classifiers, large language models, and generative models—and build trustworthy mechanisms for privacy-preserving, networked, and IoT–cloud systems.

Research interests

AI Safety, Security, and Robustness. Backdoor attacks and adversarial attacks on classifiers, LLMs, and text-to-image models.

Computer and Network Security. Privacy leakage in IoT–cloud architecture and home IoT gateways.

Data Privacy and Anonymization. Perturbation-based techniques and differential privacy system implementation.
