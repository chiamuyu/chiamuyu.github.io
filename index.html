Academic profile · 2026

Building trustworthy intelligent systems at the intersection of AI security, privacy, and networked computing.

My research examines how learning-enabled systems fail, how attackers exploit those failures, and how we can build meaningful safeguards. I work on adversarial and backdoor threats, privacy-preserving computation, and security in IoT–cloud environments.

01 — RESEARCH

AI Safety & Security

Backdoor attacks, adversarial examples, and safety risks in classifiers, large language models, and generative models.

02 — SYSTEMS

Computer & Network Security

Security and privacy leakage in IoT–cloud architectures, connected devices, and home gateways.

03 — PRIVACY

Data Privacy & Anonymization

Perturbation-based techniques, differential privacy, and practical privacy-preserving systems.
